## Subaccounts

A subaccount is a named space inside an account, used to separate money by
purpose — an emergency fund, a holiday, recurring bills, investments or a
personal goal. Moving money between subaccounts does not change the total
balance of the account.

## Allocation rules

An allocation rule splits incoming money automatically. A rule has a trigger
(for example, salary received) and a list of destinations with percentages.
Rules are executed by a deterministic rules engine, and every execution is
recorded. The assistant may suggest a rule, but a rule only becomes active
after the account holder approves it.

## What the assistant can and cannot do

The assistant explains your financial situation, analyses spending and
simulates what-if scenarios. It reads balances and transactions through the
banking services, so the figures it quotes are the real ones.

The assistant cannot move money on its own. Any operation that changes your
accounts requires your explicit confirmation and is then carried out by the
banking system, not by the assistant.
